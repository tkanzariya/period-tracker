A. Safety and medical accuracy (found by the audit; Drashti must review the wording and numbers)

M1

A late or missed period is explained badly

Critical Today, reminders 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

After 79 or 173 days with no new period, Today says "Your period is a little late. This can happen" and, next to it, "79 days later than my guess". It never mentions pregnancy, so someone could be falsely reassured.

What we will change

Wording by lateness: up to 7 days "a few days late, this can happen"; after 7 days "late. If there is any chance you are pregnant, a pregnancy test is a good next step. If it keeps being late, please talk to a doctor"; after 60 days "I have not heard about a period for N days. Did you forget to log one? If not, please see a doctor". Includes the calm "Is my period late?" list of common reasons (old idea F3). No diagnosis.

Source: audit D1, G1; plan F3

M2

Phase and "Day N" shown after a long silence

Critical Today, Calendar 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

If nothing is recorded for months, Today shows "Day 205 of your cycle" and the Luteal phase, which is made up.

What we will change

After a long silence stop showing the cycle day and phase. The cut-off depends on her age group (from M3): about 60 days for under 20 and 20 to 39, longer for 40 and over because gaps are common near menopause (numbers NEEDS DRASHTI REVIEW). Show "No period recorded for N days. Did it come?" with one button to record it. The Calendar stops drawing phases beyond that point.

Source: audit D2, E (Today), G2

M3

Teenagers and women over 40 get the same "tell your doctor" flag

High Today, My cycles, flags 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

A 42-day cycle is flagged "You may want to tell your doctor" for everyone, but cycles up to about 45 days are common in the first years of periods. Women in their 40s get the same flag with no explanation that cycles often change then.

What we will change

Ask once "How old are you?" (under 20 / 20 to 39 / 40 and over, no exact age). Under 20: the normal range is wider for the first years and the wording says so. 40 and over: one calm line "Cycles often change in your 40s", the doctor prompt stays. Also say "most adults' cycles are 21 to 35 days" instead of "most cycles". This also covers the old "life stages" idea.

Source: audit C, D3, F3, G3; plan "Later: life stages"

M4

Ovulation guessed for irregular and teenage cycles

High Today, Calendar 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

"Ovulation is likely around 28 Nov (a guess)" appears even when cycles vary from 24 to 48 days, in a country where calendar apps are sometimes used to avoid pregnancy.

What we will change

Hide the ovulation and fertile-day guess when her cycles vary by more than 7 days, or for under-20s. Otherwise say "My guess is ovulation is around 28 Nov. This can be off by days or weeks." The "not birth control" line stays.

Source: audit C, D4, E wording

M5

The next-period window is too narrow for regular users

High Prediction 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

With four identical cycles the window is only 1 day each side, so a regular user can be surprised by a period a few days early or late.

What we will change

Never narrower than 3 days each side until she has 6 or more cycles (the number is NEEDS DRASHTI REVIEW).

Source: audit C, D5, G4

M6

One odd cycle ruins the "usual cycle"

High My cycles 0.3.0 Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

After one 99-day gap, My cycles says "Usual cycle 35 days, from 18 to 99".

What we will change

Use the middle value (median) as "Typical cycle" and show a very long or short cycle as "long gap" on its own, not inside the average.

Source: audit C, E (My cycles), G6

M7

"When to see a doctor", and a heavy or long bleeding check

High New page, Settings > Good to know, day check-in 0.3.0 (the page); the check-in goes with F1 Needs Drashti Discuss

Happening now

Nothing in the app says when bleeding, pain or bleeding between periods needs a doctor. Because period end was removed on 2026-11-18, a period lasting more than 7 days cannot be noticed at all. Pads per day is still COMING SOON.

What we will change

A short "When to see a doctor" page (changing a pad every 1 to 2 hours, clots over 2.5 cm, bleeding between periods or after sex, bleeding over 7 days, severe pain, no period for 3 months). Then, with the day check-in (F1), a one-tap "heavy" and "bleeding longer than 7 days" check that turns into one plain line for the doctor report (merges old X4 heavy-bleeding helper). Keeps the COMING SOON promise for pads per day.

Source: audit C, D6, F1, G5; plan X4

M8

Mood and energy lines sound surer than the evidence

Medium Today (phase lines), Calendar day sheet 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

"Many women feel more energetic now" and "Some women feel tired or moody around now. That is normal." The second can dismiss strong premenstrual symptoms.

What we will change

"Some people feel more energetic around now; many notice nothing." and "Some people feel tired or moody before a period. If it is strong or stops you doing daily things, tell a doctor."

Source: audit C, D7, E wording

M9

Unclear and robotic sentences in the "Is that right?" and range text

Medium Add/Change period sheets, My cycles 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

"99 days from your other periods" (whose days?), "173 days later than my guess" (sounds like an error).

What we will change

"99 days since your last period started" and "No period recorded for 173 days". Hindi follows.

Source: audit E wording

M10

Check every health number with Drashti and the original papers

High (before any public release) All cycle rules (`config/cycleRules.ts`) before 0.3.0 ships to anyone but Drashti Needs Drashti Built, Drashti to review

Happening now

The 21 to 35 range, 7-day variation, 15-day and 60-day questions, 5 bleeding days, 28-day typical and the plus or minus 3 days are all unreviewed. The exact FIGO age bands for the 7 or 9-day rule could not be confirmed from the original paper; the 21 to 45 day teen range comes from secondary summaries.

What we will change

Improve the existing review sheet docs/review/cycle-review.html (no second document) so it is easy for a non-technical nurse: every number and message in plain words, a worked example with a small diagram for each rule (for example a timeline showing what the app says on day 5, 20, 35 and 70 of a cycle), and Looks right / Needs a change with a Copy button. Claude also opens the FIGO (Munro 2018) and ACOG (Committee Opinion 651) papers and records the exact figures. Nothing is called approved until she signs it. In progress.

Source: audit C, H4; open questions 1, 4

C. New features

F1

One-tap day check-in (pain, mood, symptoms)

High Today, Calendar day sheet 0.3.0 Needs Drashti Approved

Happening now

She can only write a free-text note for a day. There is no quick way to say how a day was, which is why other apps are opened daily and this one is not. Bleeding heaviness / pads per day is COMING SOON (Tirth, 2026-11-17, keep on the list).

What we will change

Icons, no typing: pain (none, some, strong), mood, and 6 to 8 symptoms from Drashti's list (cramps, headache, back pain, bloating, tender breasts, tired, acne, spotting); heaviness joins when Drashti agrees. Feeds F2, M7 and the doctor pack (X3).

Source: plan F1; open question 1p

F2

Patterns from her own data

Medium Today 0.3.0 (needs F1 data first) Needs Drashti Discuss

Happening now

Nothing is learned from what she records beyond cycle length.

What we will change

Lines such as "Cramps usually come on day 1 and 2" or "Headaches often come 2 days before your period", shown the day before they usually happen. Only after enough entries.

Source: plan F2

X1

"Will I have my period on...?" date checker

Medium New screen 0.4.0 Needs Drashti Approved

Happening now

A common real question (wedding, trip, puja, exam) has no answer in the app; other apps show one fake date months ahead.

What we will change

She picks a date; the app says Likely / Possible / Unlikely with the honest range, getting less sure the further away it is. Never suggests medicine to move a period.

Source: plan X1

X2

The app shows how good its guesses were

Medium My cycles 0.3.0 Approved

Happening now

The app never says whether its past guesses were right.

What we will change

"My last 3 guesses: on time, 1 day early, 2 days late." No popular app does this; it fits our "honest guesses" rule and builds trust.

Source: plan X2

Medium PDF report 1.0.0 Needs Drashti Later

What we will change

Add symptoms and pain per cycle, unusual cycles marked, a "questions I want to ask" box, and always write the report in English (doctors read English).

Source: plan X3; open question 5

X5

Irregular-cycle mode

Medium Today, prediction 0.4.0 (partly covered by M2, M4, M5) Needs Drashti Approved

Happening now

What we will change

When cycles vary a lot, stop showing a sure-looking window; say "your cycles vary, so anytime between..." and count the days since the last period instead.

Source: plan X5; audit A (persona C, D)

C1

Optional account and cloud backup

Medium Settings, backup to be decided Discuss

Happening now

Backup is a file she saves and restores by hand (Settings, "Save a copy"). Nothing leaves the phone and there are no accounts.

What we will change

Tirth wants an optional account so she can save her data in the cloud and reconnect it on a new phone (this replaces the rejected "monthly copy to a folder"). This changes the promise "your cycle information stays on this phone" and the zero-cost rule, so it needs a decision first: which service (for example Google sign-in with a private backup in her own Google Drive, which costs nothing and the app's maker never sees the data, or Firebase with our own servers, which has a free tier but we would hold her health data), end-to-end encryption, and the privacy wording and Play Data safety form. Claude will bring a short comparison before any building.

Source: Tirth 2026-11-21
