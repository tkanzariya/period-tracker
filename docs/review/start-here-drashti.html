Welcome to Cycle Saathi

Your friend for understanding your cycle. Made for you by Tirth.

1. Get the app

Open the download page
  1. On your phone, open the page above.
  2. Find the newest version at the top. Scroll down to Assets and tap the file that ends in .apk (it starts with CycleSaathi). It is about 14 MB.
  3. When it has downloaded, tap Open (or find it in your phone's Downloads).
  4. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, press back, then tap Install.
  5. If Google Play Protect asks to scan the app, choose Don't send.
  6. Tap Open. You will see a rose-coloured icon called Cycle Saathi.

2. The first time

  1. Your Saathi says hello and asks what to call you. Type your name.
  2. Tap My last period started on… and choose the day it started. You can step back to an earlier day with the arrows.
  3. That's it. After 2 or 3 periods, I will start guessing when the next one comes.

Everything you write stays only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere.

3. Please try these (about 10 minutes)

  • Tell it when a period started and when it ended.
  • Add a note to a day.
  • Look at the Calendar and at My cycles.
  • Go to Settings and tap Save a copy of my information. Send the file to yourself. Then try Bring my information back.
  • Try the app with your phone in dark mode.

4. Tell me what you think

Please write down, or take a screenshot of:

  • Any word you did not understand.
  • Anything that sounds wrong, unkind, or not like a nurse would say it.
  • Anything you expected to find and could not.
  • Anything that was slow, confusing, or did not work.

Then open the other file I sent, the review sheet. It lists every number and message the app uses. For each one, tick Looks right or Needs a change. At the end press Copy my answers and send them to Tirth.

5. Good to know

  • These are guesses to help you understand your body. The app cannot tell you if something is wrong, and it is not a way to prevent pregnancy.
  • When Tirth sends a new version, open the new link and install it over the old one. Your information stays.
  • To move to a new phone, tap Save a copy of my information on the old one, and Bring my information back on the new one.

Thank you for helping make this better.
