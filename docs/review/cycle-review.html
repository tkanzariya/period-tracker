Review sheet: how the app thinks

Hi Drashti! This sheet shows every number and every sentence the app uses. Nothing here is final until you say so.

For each box, tick Looks right or Needs a change. If something needs a change, write what it should be. At the end, press Copy my answers and send them to Tirth.

You do not need to know anything about computers. Just check it as a nurse.

1. How the app guesses your next period

A "cycle" is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next period.

Which past cycles it looks at

The app says / does: It uses your last 6 cycles and takes the average length.

Why: Newer cycles tell more about you than very old ones.

When it starts guessing

The app says / does: It uses her own pattern once she has 2 finished cycles (that means 3 period start dates).

Why: One cycle is not enough to see a pattern.

Before it knows her own pattern (so the app is not empty)

The app says / does: After even one period, it shows a guess from a typical 28-day cycle, as a range of 3 days before and after. It is tagged "Typical cycle" on Today and says "A guess from a typical cycle" in the Calendar. It switches to her own pattern by itself.

Why: An empty app feels useless; a clearly labelled guess is better than nothing.

The guess is a range, not one day

The app says / does: It shows a range, at least 1 day before and after the best guess. If your cycles change a lot, the range gets wider.

Why: A single exact day would look more sure than it really is.

2. What the app calls "usual"

When something is outside these numbers, the app says: "You may want to tell your doctor". It never says something is wrong.

Usual cycle length

The app says / does: 21 to 35 days.

Why: A shorter or longer cycle is mentioned in a calm message.

Usual period length

The app says / does: Up to 7 days.

If she forgets to say when the period ended

The app says / does: The app guesses the period lasted 5 days.

Cycles that change a lot

The app says / does: If her longest cycle is more than 7 days longer than her shortest cycle (out of the last 6), the app mentions it.

3. Ovulation and fertile days

All of these are guesses made from period dates only. The app says so every time.

When ovulation happens

The app says / does: 14 days before the next period starts.

Why: The time from ovulation to the next period stays fairly steady (about 12 to 14 days) even when cycles are longer or shorter.

The "Ovulation" part of the cycle

The app says / does: The ovulation day, plus 1 day before and 1 day after (so 3 days in total).

Fertile days (best chance of pregnancy)

The app says / does: From 5 days before ovulation to 1 day after it.

Why: Sperm can live up to about 5 days. The egg lives about 12 to 24 hours.

"Lower-fertility days" (hidden unless she turns it on)

The app says / does: Days outside the fertile days, with 2 days extra on each side to be careful. Only shown when her cycles are regular. Always with the words "not a way to prevent pregnancy".

Why: We do not use the words "safe period" because these guesses can be wrong.

4. Checks when she tells the app something

The app now stops mistakes and explains calmly, with a button for the next step. Numbers used: asks if a new period starts fewer than 15 days after another; asks if a period would last more than 14 days; treats a period with no end day as forgotten after 10 days and asks about it on Today.

She picks a day that has not come yet

The app says / does: "That day has not come yet". Please pick today or an earlier day.

A period already starts on that day

The app says / does: "A period already starts on 6 Oct". You have already told me about it. Button: "Change that period".

She says a period started on a day inside an earlier period

The app says / does: "That day is inside your period (6–10 Oct)". A new period cannot start while one is going on. Button: "Change that period".

She says a period started while the last one has no end day

The app says / does: "Your period from 6 Oct has not ended". Tell me when it ended first. Then you can add the new one. Button: "Tell me the end day".

The new start runs into a period that begins later

The app says / does: "This runs into your period that starts on 6 Oct". Please pick an earlier day.

A new period only a few days after another one started (the app asks if that is right)

The app says / does: "Only 14 days after another period started". Most cycles are 21 to 35 days long. Is this a new period? Buttons: "Yes, save it" / "No, go back".

She says a period ended, but none is going on

The app says / does: "No period is going on". Tell me when a period started first. Button: "Period started".

The end day is before the start day

The app says / does: "That is before the period started (6 Oct)". Please pick a day on or after it.

That period already has an end day

The app says / does: "That period already ended on 10 Oct". You can change its days if that is not right. Button: "Change that period".

The period would be very long (the app asks if that is right)

The app says / does: "That makes your period 15 days long". Is that right? Buttons: "Yes, save it" / "No, go back".

Changing a period so it overlaps another

The app says / does: "This overlaps your period from 6–10 Oct". Periods cannot overlap. Please check the days.

She saves an empty note

The app says / does: "Write something first". e.g. cramps, travelling, took medicine

The note is too long

The app says / does: "That note is too long". Please keep it under 500 letters.

5. What the Saathi says and the main button

The big button changes with her situation.

The big button

The app says / does: Before any period: "Add my last period". While a period is going on: "Period ended". Otherwise: "Period started".

Saathi before any period is told

The app says / does: Hi Drashti! Tell me the day your last period started. I will learn from it.

Saathi while a period is going on

The app says / does: Your period is here, Drashti. Be kind to yourself today. Tell me when it ends.

Saathi when a period was never ended

The app says / does: Drashti, your period started 12 days ago and I have not heard that it ended. Tell me the day it ended.

Saathi while using a typical cycle

The app says / does: Drashti, I do not know your own cycle yet, so I am using a typical 28-day cycle. Tell me about your next periods and I will learn yours.

The note on Today when a period was never ended

The app says / does: "Did your period end?" You told me it started on 3 Oct. Button: "Tell me the end day".

How the next-period guess is explained

The app says / does: I looked at your last 5 cycles. They were about 31 days long. Or: Based on a typical 28-day cycle. I will learn yours as you add periods.

6. First start, tour and report

Welcome: what it says about privacy and guesses

The app says / does: Only on your phone: Everything you tell me stays on this phone. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere. Guesses, not advice: These are only guesses. I cannot tell you if something is wrong, and I am not a way to prevent pregnancy. If you are worried, please talk to your doctor.

Tour step 1: Hello, I am your Saathi

The app says / does: I will show you around in a minute. Everything stays on your phone, and nothing here is advice. It is only guesses to help you understand your body.

Tour step 2: Today

The app says / does: This is home. The ring shows where you are in your cycle, and the colours are the parts of your cycle. Under it you see when your next period may come.

Tour step 3: The big button

The app says / does: Tap it when your period starts. While a period is going on, it changes to "Period ended". You can also add a note, or tell me about an earlier day.

Tour step 4: Calendar

The app says / does: Every day has a colour for its part of the cycle. Tap a day to see more, change a period, or write a note.

Tour step 5: My cycles

The app says / does: See how long each cycle was. You can show this page to your doctor.

Tour step 6: Keep your information safe

The app says / does: In Settings, save a copy of your information, and bring it back on a new phone. You can see this tour again there, and change the language.

The PDF report for a doctor (Settings, "Save a PDF report")

The app says / does: A branded page with her name and date, the usual cycle, shortest to longest, usual period, and a table of every period with its cycle length and notes. Footer: "Made with Cycle Saathi from what she entered herself. These are records and guesses, not a diagnosis. Please talk to a doctor about any worry."

Good to know (Settings)

The app says / does: These are only guesses to help you understand your body. This app cannot tell you if something is wrong, and it cannot stop you getting pregnant. If you are worried, please talk to your doctor.
